# Arcana_Duel_ongoing

A two-player local versus tarot fighting game made with Unity.
Current Status:Only two characters, Justice and The Fool, are available now. Additional tarot characters will be added in future updates.

Gameplay

Two players battle on the same computer. Use melee attacks, special skills and ultimate abilities to deplete the opponent’s health.
The first player to reduce the opponent’s health to zero wins. The winner proceeds to a divination screen and draws a fate tarot card.

Controls

Player 1 · Justice

• Move: A / D

• Jump: W

• Block: Hold S

• Normal Attack: F

• Special Skill: G

• Ultimate: H

Player 2 · The Fool

• Move: ← / →

• Jump: ↑

• Block: Hold ↓

• Normal Attack: K

• Special Skill: L

• Ultimate: ; (Semicolon)
